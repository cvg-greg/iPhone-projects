# iPhone-projects

## ⚡ Music GenEngine

A fully-featured, browser-based electronic music production studio —
FL-style channel rack & piano roll (snap to 1/64), Ableton-style live pattern
launching, playlist arrangement with automation, mixer with flanger / phaser /
trance gate / sidechain pump, supersaw & acid synth engines, a synthesized
drum kit, a sampler for your GarageBand / Apple Loops sounds, and offline
WAV export.

**→ Open [`Music GenEngine/index.html`](Music%20GenEngine/index.html) in your browser. That's the whole install.**

Full guide: [`Music GenEngine/README.md`](Music%20GenEngine/README.md)

### Getting it onto your Mac

Either clone this repo, or on GitHub press **Code → Download ZIP**, unzip, and
drag the `Music GenEngine` folder onto your Desktop so it lives at
`~/Desktop/Music GenEngine` — then double-click `index.html` inside it.

## 🚩 Digital Vestaboard

A digital split-flap message board — six rows of twenty-two mechanical flap
drums, rendered at 1920 × 1080 on black. Type a message and the tiles roll
through the character set until they land on it, clicking as they go. Colour
flaps, a tile painter, a rotating playlist, clock mode, and PNG export at
300 DPI.

**→ Open [`Digital Vestaboard/index.html`](Digital%20Vestaboard/index.html) in your browser.**

Full guide: [`Digital Vestaboard/README.md`](Digital%20Vestaboard/README.md)
