Time of day
Put a board up at a set time, on chosen days
Times are your computer's local clock. A rule with no days set never fires. When the page opens, whichever rule most recently passed goes up, so a display that reboots comes back to the right board.

A digital homage to the mechanical split-flap displays of railway stations — eight rows of twenty-five flap drums, each carrying a full seventy-two flap character set: blanks, A–Z, 0–9, punctuation, seven colours, black and filled.
